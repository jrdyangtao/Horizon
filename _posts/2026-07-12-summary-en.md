## [GPT-5.6 Solves 50-Year-Old Graph Theory Conjecture in Under an Hour](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/07/447873.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra autonomously proved the cycle double cover conjecture, a 50-year-old problem in graph theory, in less than one hour by using 64 parallel agents and a reduction to linear equations over finite fields. This demonstrates a major leap in AI's capability for autonomous mathematical reasoning and complex problem-solving, potentially accelerating research in automated theorem proving and mathematics. The conjecture requires that every edge in a bridgeless graph be covered exactly twice by a set of cycles. GPT-5.6's prompt specified acceptance criteria and dynamic agent allocation, and the resulting proof was just 3 pages long.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 12, 03:49

**Background**: The cycle double cover conjecture, posed independently by Szekeres and Seymour in the 1970s, states that every bridgeless graph has a collection of cycles that together include each edge exactly twice. A bridge is an edge whose removal disconnects the graph; bridgeless graphs have stronger connectivity. The conjecture is a fundamental problem in graph theory with connections to graph embeddings and the circular embedding conjecture. On July 10, 2026, OpenAI released a preprint claiming a proof generated by GPT-5.6.
